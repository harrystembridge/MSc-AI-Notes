## Kinds of Program Errors
The compiler will catch certain kinds of mistakes and will write out an error message when it finds a mistake. It will detect **syntax errors**, i.e., violations of the grammatical rules for a programming language. In this case the compiler will give you an **error message**.

Sometimes the compiler will only give you a **warning message** when you have not strictly violated the syntax rules but it is unusual enough to indicate a mistake.

Some errors are only detected by the computer when the program runs i.e. **run-time errors**.

Mistakes in the underlying algorithms themselves are known as **logic errors**. This just means that the program may not do what you want it to do, despite not throwing any errors.
