For i.i.d. observations $x_1,\dots,x_n$ with $x_i \in \{0,1\}$:

**Likelihood**
$$L(\gamma) = \prod_{i=1}^{n} p(x_i;\gamma) = \prod_{i=1}^{n} \gamma^{x_i}(1-\gamma)^{1-x_i} = \gamma^{\sum_i x_i}(1-\gamma)^{\,n-\sum_i x_i}$$

**Log-likelihood**
$$\ell(\gamma) = \log L(\gamma) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\Big[x_i \log\gamma + (1-x_i)\log(1-\gamma)\Big] = \Big(\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i\Big)\log\gamma + \Big(n-\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i\Big)\log(1-\gamma)$$

**Negative log-likelihood**
$$\mathrm{NLL}(\gamma) = -\ell(\gamma) = -\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Big[x_i \log\gamma + (1-x_i)\log(1-\gamma)\Big]$$

Setting the derivative to zero gives the MLE:
$$\hat\gamma = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i$$
