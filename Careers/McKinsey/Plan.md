## Mental maths techniques

- **Track zeros explicitly:** write 3.2bn as 3.2 × 10⁹, or keep a running units note
- **Percentages:** build from 10%, 5% and 1% (17% of 340 = 34 + 17 + 6.8)
- **Fractions:** 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/6 ≈ 16.7%, 1/7 ≈ 14.3%, 1/9 ≈ 11.1%
- **Round aggressively, and say so:** "I'll call that 50 million to keep it clean"
- **Narrate:** state the approach before calculating
- **Sanity-check every answer:** does the number make sense in the real world?

**Target:** 2-digit × 2-digit and percentage changes done cleanly, out loud, without freezing.
