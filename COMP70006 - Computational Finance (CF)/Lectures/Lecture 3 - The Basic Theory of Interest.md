> [!CARD] What is the nominal interest rate?
> The stated annual interest rate $r$, before accounting for the effect of compounding.
>

> [!CARD] What is the length of a compounding period when interest is compounded $m$ times per year?
> $1/m$ years.
>

> [!CARD] What is the interest rate for each compounding period, given nominal rate $r$ and $m$ periods per year?
> $r/m$
>

> [!CARD] What is the growth factor of an account over $k$ compounding periods?
> $\left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^k$
>

> [!CARD] What is the growth factor of an account over 1 year with $m$ compounding periods?
> $\left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^m$
>

> [!CARD] What is the effective interest rate $r_{\text{eff}}$?
> The number $r_{\text{eff}}$ such that $1 + r_{\text{eff}} = \left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^m$.
>

> [!CARD] How do you calculate the effective interest rate $r_{\text{eff}}$ from the nominal rate $r$ and $m$ periods per year?
> $r_{\text{eff}} = \left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^m - 1$

![[CF - Lecture 3.pdf#page=7|CF - Lecture 3, page 7]]

> [!CARD] How many compounding periods $k$ are there in $t$ years with $m$ periods per year?
> $k = mt$, so $\left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^k = \left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^{mt}$
>

> [!CARD] How can you rewrite the growth factor $\left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^{mt}$ in terms of the 1-year growth factor?
> $\left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^{mt} = \left(\left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^m\right)^t$
>

> [!CARD] What does the growth factor $\left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^{mt}$ converge to as $m \to \infty$ (continuous compounding)?
> $e^{rt}$
>

> [!CARD] What is the growth factor of an account after $t$ years under continuous compounding with nominal rate $r$?
> $e^{rt}$
>

> [!CARD] What limit defines $e^{rt}$ in terms of compounding?
> $e^{rt} = \lim_{m \to \infty} \left[1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right]^{mt}$
